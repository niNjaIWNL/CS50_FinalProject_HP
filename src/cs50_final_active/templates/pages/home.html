About the data

History, with context.

Insights cover January–July 2026 and include completed flights with valid scheduled and actual departure and arrival times. Cancelled flights are excluded.

A flight counts as delayed when it is more than 30 minutes late. Average minutes refer to those delayed flights. Historical performance does not guarantee your next flight's outcome.

Search with your airline's three-letter ICAO code and flight number. Airport codes, names, and cities appear automatically in the results.
